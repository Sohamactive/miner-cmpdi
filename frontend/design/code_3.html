SECTION 4.2: SEAM PROFILE & STRIPPING PARAMETERS — NORTH KARANPURA BLOCK-B

4.2.1 Detailed core exploratory drilling within the concession perimeter was completed across 48 boreholes spaced along a 400m x 400m standard exploration grid. The coal-bearing sequence conforms strictly to the Gondwana Supergroup, specifically within the Permian Barakar horizon characterized by coarse felspathic sandstones with interbedded carbonaceous shales.

verified Grounding Target • Paragraph 3 & Table 4.3

4.2.2 The operational workable seams identified within Block-B comprise Seams VII through IV. Seam VII (Top) demonstrates consistent roof stability with seam thickness ranging from 4.80 metres to 6.25 metres across boreholes NK-B-08 to NK-B-31. The major thick seam formation, Seam VI (Composite), records vertical seam thickness spanning 14.20 metres to 18.50 metres, hosting mineable clean coal horizons suitable for heavy dragline stripping.
