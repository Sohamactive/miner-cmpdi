## Brand & Style

The design system embodies the authority, structural permanence, and rigorous accountability required of national strategic mineral intelligence and public-sector energy research. Created for institutional deployment across the Central Mine Planning & Design Institute (CMPDI) and Coal India Limited (CIL) under the Ministry of Coal, the aesthetic blends modern GovTech utility with sovereign dignity.

The design movement is **Corporate / Modern** anchored by architectural precision and high-density legibility. The interface prioritizes rapid knowledge extraction, spatial predictability, and absolute data integrity over decorative embellishment. The emotional signature communicates sovereignty, scientific neutrality, non-repudiation, and operational reliability. Visual artifacts leverage crisp division of space, micro-ruled architectural dividers, and an unambiguous information hierarchy tuned for long analytic work shifts.

## Colors

The system employs a strict light-mode default calibrated to meet WCAG AAA contrast criteria across operational dashboards, dense geological metadata registries, and document analytical feeds.

- **Primary (`#0F294A`):** Deep Navy Blue. Used for executive mastheads, primary navigational scaffolding, focal action buttons, and dominant branding anchor points. Expresses legal sovereignty and operational security.
- **Secondary (`#C5832B`):** Muted Indian Ochre / Mineral Gold. Allocated to analytical highlights, critical geological classifications, advisory warnings, and statutory alert badges.
- **Tertiary (`#15803D`):** Deep Forest Emerald. Reserved strictly for verified strata logs, authenticated extraction signatures, valid statutory compliance stamps, and safe parameters.
- **Neutral & Surfaces:**
  - Base Ground: Crisp Institutional Slate (`#F8FAFC`).
  - Elevated Surfaces & Sheets: Pure White (`#FFFFFF`).
  - Structural Grids & Dividers: Muted Cool Gray (`#E2E8F0`).
  - Heavy Data Borders: Slate Gray (`#CBD5E1`).
  - Body Typography: Slate Charcoal (`#0F172A`), providing an assertive 14:1+ contrast ratio against white surfaces.
  - Secondary Metadata / Captions: Dense Slate (`#475569`).
  - Institutional Red Alert: Crimson Ochre (`#B91C1C`) for rejected, revoked, or hazardous data flags.

## Typography

The typography standard utilizes **Public Sans** for all institutional, structural, and numerical layouts due to its uncompromised clarity, uniform metric geometry, and institutional authority. For technical drill-hole identifiers, geospatial coordinates, and cryptographic repository hashes, **JetBrains Mono** is specified as the secondary monospaced utility face.

Section headings, portal sub-headers, and formal card dividers mandate uppercase letterforms using the `label-caps` role with tracked letter spacing (`+0.08em`). Numerical datasets presented in tabular matrices must use tabular figures (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`) to preserve absolute vertical column alignment across comparative geological metrics.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon a **Fluid Grid** structure anchored within a rigid max-width envelope of 1600px for panoramic analytical workstations, collapsing systematically to responsive viewports.

- **Desktop (1200px+):** 12-column grid, `gutter: 1.5rem`, `margin: 2rem`. Supports asymmetric dual-pane and tri-pane operational layouts (e.g., query rail 3 cols, data table 6 cols, analytical inspector 3 cols).
- **Tablet (768px – 1199px):** 8-column grid, `gutter: 1rem`, `margin: 1.5rem`. Collapses auxiliary inspection drawers into contextual slide-overs.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column grid, `gutter-mobile: 0.75rem`, `margin-mobile: 1rem`. Stacks horizontal split-panes vertically; sticky actions anchor to bottom utility bars.

Vertical and component spacing operates on a deterministic 4px base cadence (0.25rem). Dense data arrays inside tables and query filter sets adopt compact horizontal spacing (`space-sm` to `space-md`), minimizing unnecessary vertical scrolling during analytical sessions.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses **Low-contrast outlines** paired with disciplined **Tonal layers**. Atmospheric dropshadows and blurred glass effects are forbidden to preserve institutional austerity and CPU efficiency on enterprise terminals.

- **Level 0 (Canvas):** Pure `#F8FAFC` base page backdrop.
- **Level 1 (Panels & Workspaces):** Pure `#FFFFFF` surface bordered with a 1px solid hairline (`#E2E8F0`). Flat surface without shadow.
- **Level 2 (Dropdowns, Floating Toolbars, Pinned Headers):** `#FFFFFF` surface with a 1px border (`#CBD5E1`) and a functional micro-shadow: `0 2px 4px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.06), 0 1px 2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`.
- **Level 3 (Modal Dialogues, Critical Confirmation Overlays):** `#FFFFFF` surface with a dual border treatment (`1px solid #94A3B8`) backed by a non-blurred administrative scrim (`rgba(15, 41, 74, 0.45)`) and depth shadow: `0 10px 15px -3px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.1), 0 4px 6px -4px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08)`.

## Shapes

The design system enforces a **Soft (`1`)** shape geometry. Buttons, input fields, structural cards, and data badges apply a restrained radius of 0.25rem (4px). High-order containers (e.g., executive preview frames, repository modals) use 0.5rem (8px). 

Fully circular or pill-shaped containers are disallowed for action triggers, preserving an architectural, engineered profile suited for a sovereign ministry repository. Rounded shapes are strictly limited to circular status pips and profile micro-avatars.

## Components

### 1. Sovereign Masthead & Institutional Identity
- **National Emblem Bar:** Full-width `#0F294A` band, 56px height. Left-aligned bilingual typography ("Government of India / Ministry of Coal") accompanied by the official emblem motif. Right-aligned session security stamps and language switcher.
- **Portal Nav-Strip:** Integrated `#091E38` secondary bar with 44px tabs; active indicator denoted by a 3px solid `#C5832B` bottom border.

### 2. Buttons & Action Triggers
- **Primary Action:** Solid `#0F294A` background, `#FFFFFF` label, 1px solid `#0F294A`. Hover: `#1E3A8A`. Height: 36px (compact) or 40px (default). Focus: 2px offset ring in `#C5832B`.
- **Secondary / Technical Action:** White surface, `#0F294A` label, 1px solid `#CBD5E1`. Hover: `#F1F5F9` background, `#0F172A` border.
- **Destructive Action:** `#FFFFFF` surface, `#B91C1C` border and label. Hover: `#B91C1C` background with `#FFFFFF` label.

### 3. Data Integrity Badges & Status Chips
Badges use 0.25rem radius, uppercase 11px semi-bold lettering (`label-caps`), and a 1px border.
- **Ingested:** `#F1F5F9` fill, `#475569` text, `#CBD5E1` border.
- **In-Review:** `#FEF3C7` fill, `#92400E` text, `#FDE68A` border.
- **Verified / Approved:** `#DCFCE7` fill, `#15803D` text, `#BBF7D0` border.
- **Flagged / Rejected:** `#FEE2E2` fill, `#991B1B` text, `#FECACA` border.

### 4. Input Fields & Query Filters
- **Text Inputs:** Height 36px, `#FFFFFF` background, 1px solid `#CBD5E1` outline, 4px corner radius. Typed text is `#0F172A`. Placeholder in `#94A3B8`. Active focus shifts border to `#0F294A` with a 1px solid outline glow in `#0F294A`.
- **Segmented / Split Query Panels:** Gray container (`#F1F5F9`) with white segmented radio pills, separated by 1px `#E2E8F0` vertical rules.

### 5. Structured Data Tables
- **Table Container:** Pure white card with 1px solid `#E2E8F0` outer edge.
- **Header Row:** Subtle slate ground `#F8FAFC`, uppercase 11px tracking text `#475569`, border-bottom 2px solid `#CBD5E1`.
- **Data Rows:** 40px density row height, alternating row striping (`#FFFFFF` to `#FAFBFD`), bottom border 1px solid `#F1F5F9`. Hover state shifts row to `#F0F4F8`.

### 6. Document Preview Frames & Metadata Cards
- **Inspection Card:** Pure white ground, partitioned into top metadata grid and bottom preview canvas by a 1px `#E2E8F0` horizontal divider.
- **Breadcrumbs:** Structured breadcrumbs anchored at page top: `Repository / CMPDI Regional Institute-II / Stratum Analysis / Core Log #4092-B`, with inactive roots in `#64748B` and the active leaf in `#0F172A` bold.
